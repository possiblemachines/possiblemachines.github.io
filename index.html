>POSSIBLE MACHINES_

"Cybernetics...takes as its subject-matter the domain of 'all possible machines', and is only secondarily interested if informed that some of them have not yet been made, either by Man or by Nature. "
-- W. Ross Ashby, An Introduction to Cybernetics, p.2

[possible machines] is an informal community gathering series i.e 'salon' for creative technologists, media artists, and computational designers in Bangalore, creating a space for regular meetups, gallery exhibitions, niche workshops, exchange of ideas, and the discussion of emerging practices across art, technology, design and computation.

It is part of creativetech.india, a community of practitioners, researchers and professional as well as a creative directory dedicated to publishing, presenting, and platforming creative technology within India.
